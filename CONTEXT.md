# CONTEXT.md — Vocabulary & Domain Ontology Diary

## 1. Domain Entities & Vocabulary

- **Coffee Science Deep Research**: Comprehensive scientific analysis translating peer-reviewed food chemistry, physics, and sensory analysis to specialty coffee roasting and brewing practices.
- **Oral Tribology**: The study of friction and lubrication between the tongue, palate, and beverage, dictating mouthfeel and astringency.
- **Astringency (ความฝาดแห้ง)**: Tactile sensation of puckering/roughness mediated by the interaction of phenolic-rich compounds with salivary proline-rich proteins (PRPs).
- **Melanoidins (เมลาโนอิดิน)**: High- and low-molecular weight brown nitrogenous polymers formed via the Maillard reaction. Low-molecular weight phenolic-rich melanoidins are the primary drivers of coffee astringency.
- **Acrylamide (อะคริลาไมด์ - $C_3H_5NO$)**: Process contaminant (IARC Group 2A probable carcinogen) generated during coffee roasting through reaction of 5-HMF and free asparagine.
- **5-HMF (5-Hydroxymethylfurfural)**: Predominant carbonyl precursor derived from sucrose pyrolysis, driving the bell-shaped kinetic curve of acrylamide formation.
- **Q-Arabica Grader**: Licensed cupping specialist accredited by the Coffee Quality Institute (CQI) to evaluate specialty coffee quality.
- **SCA Coffee Body**: Quantitative and qualitative mouthfeel attribute defined by thickness, mouthcoating, and tactile texture.

## 2. Taxonomy Tag System

- `#SensoryScience`: Sensory evaluation, flavor profiles, and cupping scores.
- `#RoastingChemistry`: Thermodynamics, heat transfer, First Crack, and development time kinetics.
- `#MaillardReaction`: Non-enzymatic browning between amino acids and reducing/reactive carbohydrates.
- `#SpecialtyCoffee`: Specialty grade coffees scoring $\ge 80$ SCA points.
- `#FoodSafety`: Chemical contaminants, safe consumption limits, and mitigation strategies.
- `#KineticModeling`: Multiresponse kinetic differential equations modeling chemical flux during thermal treatment.
- `#Extraction`: Brewing variables (temperature, contact time, grind size, yield).
